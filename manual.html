Getting Started
Open the app and choose your store
🌐 Opening the App
  1. On your phone or tablet, open your browser and go to the Deli Pro URL your coordinator gave you.
  2. You'll see a store picker screen. Tap the dropdown and choose your store number and city.
  3. Tap Open Store — the app loads your live Google Sheet data.
  4. Bookmark the page so you can return with one tap.
💡
Pro tip: On iPhone, tap the share icon → "Add to Home Screen." On Android, tap the browser menu → "Add to Home Screen." The app will behave just like a native app.
🔗 Direct Store Link

Once you've opened your store at least once, you can bookmark the direct link which includes your store number:

https://yoursite.com/?store=127

Replace 127 with your store number.

ℹ️
What this app does: Deli Pro connects directly to your store's Google Sheet. Every count you enter, every invoice you log — it saves straight to the sheet in real time. No separate login required.

Inventory Count Sheet
Enter on-hand counts for every item
📦 What You'll See

When you tap Inventory, the app loads your store's count sheet directly from Google Sheets. You'll see a table with these columns:

ColumnWhat it means
Count ByThe unit you're counting — CASE, EACH, BAG, etc.
ItemThe full item name exactly as it appears in the BEK catalog
Item #The BEK item number (used to match prices automatically)
Case PackPack size, e.g. 2/5Lb
On Hand✏️ This is the field you type in — your current count
PerCost per unit. Updates automatically when you use the Purch? feature
TotalOn Hand × Per — your inventory value for that row
📦 Purch?Check this box for any item you received from BEK this week
✏️ How to Enter Counts
  1. Tap the Inventory sub-tab. The sheet loads in a few seconds.
  2. Find the item you want to count. Items are grouped by section (blue header rows).
  3. Tap the white number box in the On Hand column for that item.
  4. Type your count. You can use decimals (e.g. 2.5 for half a case).
  5. Press Enter or tap somewhere else — the app saves that one cell immediately. The border flashes green to confirm.
  6. Repeat for all items.
  7. When you're done, tap 💾 Save Count & Update Purchased Prices to save everything at once.
💡
Save as you go, or save at the end. Each box auto-saves when you tap away from it. The "Save Count & Update Purchased Prices" button saves all boxes in one shot — useful if you've been going quickly and want to make sure nothing was missed.
⚠️
Don't skip items. If an item is truly out of stock, enter 0 — don't leave the box blank. The analytics and food-cost calculations depend on having a real number in every row.

BEK Purchased Prices
Update costs automatically for items you received this week
🔄 Why This Exists

BEK (Ben E. Keith) prices change weekly. Instead of manually looking up and typing in each price, the app pulls the current cost automatically — but only for the items you actually received that week. That way you're not updating prices on items you didn't buy.

  1. Enter your on-hand counts as you normally would (see Section 3).
  2. Walk through the sheet. For any item you received a BEK delivery on this week, check the 📦 Purch? checkbox at the end of that row.
  3. When you're done checking all received items, tap 💾 Save Count & Update Purchased Prices.
  4. The app saves all your counts first, then fetches the current BEK price for each checked item, writes the new cost into the Per column, and clears all the checkboxes. Done.
ℹ️
How prices get there: Your coordinator updates the BEK List in the coordinator spreadsheet once a week, then runs a script that pushes all prices to a shared price feed. When your store saves Purch? items, it reads directly from that feed — no manual entry needed on your end.
⚠️
"X items not in BEK feed" — If you see this warning after saving, it means that item number wasn't found in the coordinator's price list. Ask your coordinator to add it to the BEK List tab. The count was still saved — only the price update was skipped.
🚫
Only check Purch? for items you actually received. If you check an item you didn't buy this week, the price gets overwritten with the latest BEK price, which may not match your actual invoice cost.
5
Count Log
Record individual count entries with date, section, and flag
📓 What It Does

The Count Log lets you record a timestamped count entry for any single item — with its section, category, and status flag (OK / LOW / OUT). Entries go into the COUNT HISTORY tab in your Google Sheet, building a running audit trail.

➕ Logging a Count Entry
  1. Tap Count Log.
  2. The Date field defaults to today — change it only if you're backdating an entry.
  3. Choose the item from the Item dropdown (pulled from your Inventory tab).
  4. Select the correct Section — Deli, Fountain, or Branded Deli.
  5. Pick the Category that fits (Bread, Cheese, Deli Meat, etc.).
  6. Type the On Hand Count.
  7. Set the Flag:
    • OK — adequate stock
    • LOW — getting close, reorder soon
    • OUT — zero stock, needs immediate attention
  8. Tap Log Count.
💡
Load Inventory first. The item dropdown on Count Log is populated from your Inventory tab. If you open Count Log before Inventory, the dropdown may be empty. Just tap Inventory first, then come back.

Food Cost Calculator
Track weekly food cost percentage against your 25% target
📊 How Food Cost Works

Food Cost % = COGS ÷ Weekly Sales × 100. COGS is your beginning inventory value plus all purchases. The app calculates everything automatically — you just fill in the numbers.

≤25%
On Target ✓
25–30%
Watch It ⚠
>30%
Over Budget ✗
📝 Logging a Weekly Entry
  1. Tap Food Cost.
  2. Set the Week End Date to the Saturday of the week you're closing out.
  3. Enter Weekly Sales ($) from your POS report.
  4. Under Beginning Inventory, enter the dollar value for Deli, Fountain, and Branded Deli separately.
  5. Under Purchases, enter what you spent this week on Hunt Brothers, Icee, and Ben E. Keith.
  6. COGS and Food Cost % calculate instantly — no math needed.
  7. Add any Notes (holiday week, promotion, unusual variance).
  8. Tap Save Entry.
💡
The top of the screen shows your 8-week rolling average so you can spot trends at a glance. If you're consistently over 25%, that's a sign to review portion sizes, waste, or ordering patterns.

Invoices
Log every received invoice in seconds
🧾 Logging an Invoice
  1. Tap Invoices.
  2. Set the Date Received to today (or the delivery date).
  3. Type the Vendor name (Sysco, BEK, Hunt Brothers, etc.).
  4. Enter the Invoice # from the paper invoice.
  5. Enter the Amount ($) — the invoice total.
  6. In Items / Description, briefly note what was delivered (a short description is fine).
  7. Use Notes for any issues: substitutions, shortages, damaged goods.
  8. Tap Log Invoice.
💡
The header shows a This Month running total — a quick check to make sure your spending is tracking to budget before the month closes.
⚠️
Log invoices the day they arrive. Waiting until the end of the week means details get fuzzy — what was substituted, what was short, etc. Two minutes when the driver hands you the invoice saves headaches later.
8
Orders
Track pending and placed orders
📋 Placing or Logging an Order
  1. Tap Orders.
  2. Fill in Date, Vendor, Item, Unit, and Quantity.
  3. Set the Status:
    • Pending — you know you need it, haven't called yet
    • Ordered — the order is placed
    • Received — delivery confirmed
    • Cancelled — order was cancelled
    • Back-Order — vendor is out of stock
  4. Add any Notes (special handling, substitution instructions).
  5. Tap Save Order.
ℹ️
Open orders (Pending, Ordered, Back-Order) appear in their own Open Orders card at the top of the list so nothing slips through the cracks.

Recipes
Build recipe costing sheets — cost per serving, total cost
👨‍🍳 Adding a Recipe Ingredient
  1. Tap Recipes, then tap + Add.
  2. Type the Recipe Name (e.g. Club Sandwich). Use the same name for every ingredient in one recipe — they group together automatically.
  3. Choose the Category (Sandwiches, Hot Foods, etc.).
  4. Enter the Servings / Yield for this recipe (e.g. 10 sandwiches from one batch).
  5. Enter one Ingredient name, Quantity, and Unit.
  6. Enter the Cost per Unit — the Ext. Cost calculates automatically.
  7. Tap Save Ingredient. Repeat for each ingredient.

Once you've added all ingredients, the recipe card shows Total Cost and Cost per Serving automatically.

💡
Recipe costing pays off when your food cost % creeps up. If your Club Sandwich was costing $1.80/serving last quarter and it's now $2.20, you know it's time to review your pricing or portion sizes.

Suppliers
Your vendor contact directory
📞 Adding a Supplier
  1. Tap Suppliers, then tap + Add Supplier.
  2. Enter the Supplier Name, Rep Name, Phone, and Email.
  3. Set the Delivery Day from the dropdown.
  4. Add any Notes (minimum orders, lead times, special items they carry).
  5. Tap Save Supplier.

Phone numbers and email addresses in the list are tappable — tap a phone number to call, tap an email to send a message.

Inspection
Complete a scored food-safety walkthrough
✅ Running an Inspection
  1. Tap the Inspect module (bottom nav or top tab).
  2. Work through each section — Storerooms, Refrigerator, Freezers, Inventory, Pizza Station, 3-Compartment Sink, and more.
  3. For each checklist item, tap:
    • YES — compliant
    • NO — not compliant (counts against your score)
    • N/A — not applicable today
  4. When all sections are done, review your score and fill in the Inspector name, Follow-up Date, and any Notes.
  5. Tap Save Inspection — it writes to your Google Sheet's Inspections tab.
  6. Optionally tap Export PDF to save or share a formatted report.

📝 Completing a Log
  1. Tap the Maintenance module.
  2. Choose the piece of equipment from the list.
  3. Work through the checklist — tap each task to mark it complete.
  4. Fill in any temperature or PPM readings if required.
  5. Enter your initials and confirm the date and time.
  6. Tap Submit Log.
🚫
Do not skip temperature logs. Refrigerator must read 41°F or below. Freezer must read 0°F or below. Ice cream machine hot-gas cycle must be performed weekly. These are health-code requirements, not suggestions.

Coordinator View
Chain-wide dashboard — managers only
🗂 Accessing the Coordinator Dashboard

The Coordinator view is a separate screen for the Food & Beverage Coordinator. It shows every store's inspection status and food cost in one table.

https://yoursite.com/?view=coordinator

Or tap the "🗂 Coordinator View" link at the bottom of the store picker screen.

FeatureDescription
Inspection SummaryEvery store's last inspection date, score, number of NOs, follow-up due date, and status (Clear / Pending / Overdue)

Export Report PDFOne-tap chain-wide inspection PDF for your records
Backup All StoresCreates a dated snapshot of every store's Inventory tab — keeps the last 4 weekly backups
💰 Updating BEK Prices (Coordinator Only)
  1. Open the Coordinator spreadsheet in Google Sheets.
  2. Go to the BEK List tab and update item prices for this week.
  3. Open Extensions → Apps Script and run pushBEKPricesToAllStores().
  4. The script pushes all prices to the shared feed. All 43 stores will pull current prices automatically when their staff saves Purch? items.
💡
Set a weekly trigger in Apps Script (Triggers → Time-driven → Week → Monday morning) so this runs automatically without you having to remember.

Troubleshooting
When something doesn't look right
🔧 Common Issues
ProblemFix
Inventory tab is empty Your store's Google Sheet hasn't been shared with the service account. Contact your coordinator — they need to share the sheet with the app's service account email.
"Could not load data from Google Sheets" Check your internet connection. If it's good, the Sheets API may be rate-limited — wait 30 seconds and try again. If the error persists, contact your coordinator.
The save button border turns red The individual count save failed. Try tapping away from the cell and back again. If still failing, use "Save Count & Update Purchased Prices" to save all at once.
"BEK price feed not configured" The Supabase credentials haven't been added to serviceAccount.js. Ask whoever manages the app deployment to add SB_URL and SB_KEY.
"X items not in BEK feed" Those item numbers aren't in the coordinator's BEK List tab. Ask the coordinator to add them. Counts were still saved — only the price update was skipped.
Count Log item dropdown is empty Navigate to the Inventory tab first (it loads the item list). Then come back to Count Log.
Analytics shows no data Visit Inventory, Food Cost, Invoices, and Orders tabs first, then return to Analytics. It only shows data loaded in the current session.
Store picker doesn't show my store Your store number may not be in the app's store list yet. Contact your coordinator — they'll need to add it to stores.js.
App shows "Setup Required" The Google Service Account credentials aren't configured. This is a deployment issue — contact whoever manages the Vercel hosting.
📡 App Doesn't Need the Internet — Except to Save

You can type counts into the boxes without a connection — but nothing saves to the sheet until you're online. If you're in a dead zone, complete your counts and wait until you have signal before tapping Save.
