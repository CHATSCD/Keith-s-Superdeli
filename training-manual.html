Chapter One

Getting Started — Opening the App

Deli Pro runs entirely in a web browser. There's nothing to install. You can use it on any phone, tablet, or computer as long as you have internet access.

How to Open Your Store

1
Open a browser

Chrome, Safari, or Edge all work. Type the store's URL into the address bar or tap the bookmark your lead set up.

2
Select your store

You'll see a store-picker screen with the Keith's logo. Tap the dropdown, find your store number and city, then tap Open Store. You can also double-tap your store in the dropdown to go directly.

3
The app loads your store's data

The header will show your store number and city (e.g., #107 — Saucier). You're now connected to your store's live Google Sheet. Every change you make saves there instantly.

💡
Bookmark it. Once your store is open, bookmark the page. Next time you open the bookmark, it goes straight to your store — no picker needed.

What You'll See on the Store Picker

Keith's Superstores — Deli Pro
KEITH'S SUPERSTORES
Select your store to open Deli Pro
▾  #107 — Saucier
Open Store →
🗂 Coordinator View

If You See "Service Account Not Configured"

This means the app hasn't been set up with Google credentials yet. This is an admin issue — contact your Food & Beverage Coordinator. You cannot fix this yourself, and it's nothing you did wrong.

If You See "Unknown Store"

The store number in the URL doesn't match the store list. Go back to the store picker and select your store from the dropdown instead of typing the URL manually.

Chapter Three

Daily Inventory Form

The Daily Inventory Form tracks your department's inventory costs every day of the week. It covers three sections of the deli: Deli, Branded Deli, and Beverage Station.

📅
This form runs Sunday through Saturday. Each week is its own form. The form defaults to the current week automatically.

The Three Sections

SectionWhat It Covers
🔵 Deli InventoryHot Deli, Hotdog, Chili Cheese, Peanut Patch
🟣 Branded DeliHunt Brothers, Piccadilly Pizza
🟦 Beverage StationCoffee, Cappuccino, Fountain Drinks, BIBs, Frozen Drinks, Tea

The Five Rows (Per Section)

Each section has five rows. You fill in a dollar amount for each day of the week (Sun–Sat):

RowSymbolWhat to Enter
Beginning Inv.$Dollar value of inventory at the start of the day
Purchases+Dollar value of product received that day
% of Retail Sales−Percentage of retail sales (your lead will tell you this number)
Transfers+/−Product moved in or out from another department or store
Ending Inv.(auto)Calculated automatically — you do not enter this
⚠️
Do not type in the Ending Inv. row. It calculates itself from the other four rows. If it looks wrong, check your other entries first.

Step-by-Step: Filling Out the Form

1
Go to Deli Pro → Daily Inv. Form

The current week auto-loads. If you need a different week, use the Week of (Sunday) date picker at the top and tap Load Week.

2
Fill in each column for today's date

Find today's day column (Sun / Mon / Tue…) and enter the dollar amounts for Beginning Inv., Purchases, % of Retail Sales, and Transfers for each section.

3
Tap 💾 Save All Sections

This saves all three sections at once. You'll see a green confirmation when it's saved.

Other Buttons on This Screen

ButtonWhat It DoesWho Uses It
🖨 Print FormOpens a print preview of the inventory formAnyone
📥 Pull Week for Store…Loads another store's weekly data for comparisonLeads / Coordinator
🔒 Close WeekLocks the week permanently — requires your lead's PINLeads only
🔴
🔒 Close Week cannot be undone. Once closed, no one can edit that week's data. Only leads should use this button, and only after verifying all entries are correct.

Lead PIN Registration

If your store has no leads registered, a Register Your Lead PIN banner appears at the top. Leads must register here before they can close a week or sign forms. Fill out the name, email, create a 4–6 digit PIN, confirm it, select the role, and tap Register.

Chapter Four

Inventory Counts

The Inventory tab is where you record how much of each item you have on hand. It connects directly to your store's Google Sheet — every number you enter is saved live.

Inventory Sub-Tabs

Depending on your store's setup, you'll see either a single tab or multiple tabs at the top of the inventory list:

All Items 🥩 Deli 🍕 Branded Deli ☕ Fountain

Start with All Items to see everything at once. Use the individual tabs to focus on one section at a time.

How to Enter a Count

1
Find the item in the list

Items are grouped by section (Deli, Branded Deli, Fountain) with a colored header row separating each group.

2
Tap the On Hand column for that item

The cell will become active (blue border = ready to type).

3
Type the count and tap Enter or tap away

The app auto-saves the moment you leave the field. Watch for the color feedback: blue border = saving… → green = saved ✓ → back to normal.

The Inventory Columns Explained

ColumnWhat It Means
Count ByUnit to count in (lbs, cases, each, etc.) — do not change this
ItemProduct name
Item #Vendor item number for ordering
Case PackHow many units come in one case
On HandYour current count — this is the only column you edit
PerUnit cost per item (auto-filled from BEK price feed)
TotalAuto-calculated: On Hand × Per
📦 Purch?Checkbox — mark items you want to pull prices for from the BEK price feed
⚠️
Check the "Count By" column before entering a number. If an item is counted in cases but you enter individual units, your total will be way off. When in doubt, ask your lead.

OUT / LOW Status Indicators

Some stores track item status. Rows may be highlighted:

OUT LOW OK

The sub-tab buttons show a count of how many items are currently OUT. If you see a red number on the "All Items" tab, those items need to be ordered or restocked immediately.

Uploading BEK Prices

Leads can upload a price CSV from Ben E. Keith to automatically update the "Per" (unit price) column for checked items.

1
Check the 📦 Purch? box for every item you want to price-update
2
Tap 📤 Upload BEK Prices

A panel slides open. The CSV file must have columns named item_num and price (or Item Number / Unit Price).

3
Tap Process & Upload

The app matches items by item number and updates prices. You'll see a summary: how many counts saved, prices updated, and items not found in the feed.

🚫
Never delete rows from the inventory list. Deleting rows breaks the formulas in the Google Sheet. If an item needs to be removed or added, contact your lead or coordinator.

🚨

Chapter Nine

Labels — Printing Product Date Labels

Every prepped item leaving your counter must have a label with the product name, prep date, and use-by date. The Labels tab makes this fast and consistent.

How to Print a Label

1
Go to Deli Pro → Labels

The Labels tool opens inside the app (it loads as a full-screen panel).

2
Select the product

Choose from the list or type the product name.

3
Enter the prep date and use-by date

Today's date fills in automatically. Adjust the use-by date based on your product's shelf life guidelines.

4
Print

Send to your label printer. The label will show the product name, prep date/time, and use-by date clearly.

🏷️
No label = no product on the floor. A product without a date label is treated as unidentified during inspections and must be discarded. Label everything, every time.
💡
Labels can also be accessed directly by going to /labels.html in your browser. Bookmark it on the prep station tablet for quick access without navigating the full app.

Chapter Ten

Food Safety Inspections

The Inspection module is used to conduct formal food safety audits of the deli department. It walks through 14 sections and over 150 questions. Each answer is yes, no, or N/A — with photos required whenever the answer is NO.

🚨
Every blank answer is treated as a failure on the Coordinator Dashboard. Every section must have an answer, even if the answer is N/A.

Step 1 — Fill In the Inspection Details

Before answering any questions, complete the header fields:

FieldWhat to Enter
Inspector NameYour full name — required
DateToday's date — auto-fills, adjust if needed
On ScheduleNames of staff currently on the schedule
Kitchen StaffNames of kitchen staff on duty right now
Front StaffNames of front-of-house staff on duty
Staffing NeededAny open positions or staffing gaps

Step 2 — Answer All 14 Sections

Scroll through each section. For every question, tap one of three buttons:

YES — Compliant NO — Issue Found N/A — Not Applicable

The section badge at the top of each section updates in real time:

Pending — not fully answered Pass — all YES Issues Found — one or more NOs

A progress bar at the top of the page shows how many of the 150 questions you've answered.

Step 3 — Attach Photos for Every NO

When you tap NO on any question, a photo upload area appears below that question automatically. You must attach at least one photo documenting the issue. Tap the file/camera area and take a photo directly or upload from your camera roll.

📷
Photos are your proof. They protect you, document the issue, and give the coordinator the context to help fix it remotely.

Ice Cream Machine — Maintenance Ticket

If you mark NO for the ice cream machine question, a Maintenance Ticket form automatically appears. Fill in:

FieldWhat to Enter
Ticket NumberAuto-generated (MNT-XXXXXX) — do not change
Reported ByYour name
PriorityNormal / High / Urgent
EquipmentPre-filled as "Ice Cream Machine" — adjust if needed
Issue DescriptionDescribe exactly what's wrong in detail

Tap Generate Ticket. The ticket is included in the saved inspection and the PDF export automatically.

Step 4 — Add Any Notes

At the bottom of the inspection there's an Additional Notes text area. Use this for anything you observed that doesn't fit into the checklist questions — context, explanations, follow-up info.

Step 5 — Save & Export

1
Tap "Save Inspection to Sheet"

Saves the full inspection result to your store's Google Sheet including your score %, YES/NO/N/A counts, list of all NO items, and follow-up date.

2
Tap "Export PDF"

Downloads a formatted inspection report PDF with the store info, section results, all NO items highlighted, maintenance ticket (if applicable), notes, and signature lines. File saves as Inspection_Store[#]_[date].pdf.

Follow-Up Date

If the inspection has any NO answers, a Follow-Up Required banner appears with a follow-up date set 7 days from the inspection date. The coordinator tracks these — stores with overdue follow-ups appear in red on the coordinator dashboard.

The 14 Inspection Sections

Storerooms Refrigerator Freezer Cooking Equipment Hot Hold Cold Hold Ice Cream Machines Personal Hygiene Chemicals & Cleaning Pest Control Labeling & Dating Cross-Contamination Allergen Awareness Overall Facility

Chapter Twelve

Temperature Logs

Temperature logs are required every 4 hours. If you're on a standard shift, that means you'll take readings at least twice — once when you arrive and once before you leave.

Refrigerator Temp Log

Acceptable range: 35°F – 41°F

Units tracked:

Walk-in Cooler Deli Reach-in

Time slots: 6 AM · 10 AM · 2 PM · 6 PM · 10 PM · 2 AM

Freezer Temp Log

Acceptable range: ≤ 0°F (negative)

Units tracked:

Walk-in Freezer Display Freezer Ice Cream Freezer

Same time slots as refrigerator.

How to Log a Temperature Reading

1
Go to Deli Logs → Fridge Temps or Freezer Temps
2
Find the current time slot row

The time slots run down the left side of the table. Find the row closest to your current time.

3
Enter the temperature for each unit

Tap the temperature field, type the reading (decimals allowed, e.g., 38.5). If the number is out of range, a red alert box appears immediately.

4
Enter your initials in the Initials column

Max 5 characters. This records who took the reading.

5
Complete the 5 Daily Tasks checkboxes below the table

These confirm you checked door seals, verified thermometers, alerted the lead if needed, etc.

6
Tap Save and/or Export PDF
🚨
Out-of-range reading? Act immediately.
  • Notify your lead right away — do not wait
  • Move at-risk product to a working unit immediately
  • Product in the danger zone (41°F–135°F) for more than 4 hours must be discarded
  • Document the issue and your corrective action in the Notes field

Chapter Fifteen

Common Errors & How to Fix Them

Error / ProblemWhat It MeansWhat to Do
"Service Account Not Configured" The app hasn't been connected to Google by an admin yet Contact your Food & Beverage Coordinator — not a user fix
"Unknown Store" The store number in the URL doesn't exist in the system Go back to the store picker and select your store from the dropdown
"No Sheet ID configured" Your store's Google Sheet hasn't been linked Contact your coordinator to run the migration setup
Red error on saving a count Save failed — usually a network or permissions issue Check your internet, try again. If it keeps failing, the service account may not be shared on the sheet — contact coordinator
Tab missing (auto-created) A sheet tab didn't exist — the app created it automatically Normal behavior. The tab is now there — reload and try again
Ending Inv. shows wrong number One of the other four rows has a wrong entry Check Beginning Inv., Purchases, % of Retail Sales, and Transfers — the Ending Inv. row is calculated, not entered
BEK upload shows "X items not in BEK feed" Those items' item numbers don't match the BEK CSV Check that the item numbers in the inventory list match what's in the CSV exactly
Inspection not saving — "Please enter inspector name" The Inspector Name field at the top is blank Scroll to the top of the inspection and fill in the Inspector Name field
App loads very slowly Large amount of data in the Google Sheet, or slow internet Be patient on first load — data is pulled live. Subsequent loads in the same session are faster
Close Week button is disabled / not visible No lead PIN is registered for your store, or there are no NO entries to trigger the confirmation Register a lead PIN in the Daily Inv. Form banner first
💡
When in doubt, refresh the page and try again. Most one-off errors resolve with a fresh load. If an error persists across multiple attempts, document exactly what you were doing and contact your coordinator.
